# Privacy Policy — AnyData

**Last updated:** 2026-08-12  
**App:** AnyData - Design your database (`com.losiu.anydata`)  
**Contact:** [cslosiu@gmail.com](mailto:cslosiu@gmail.com)

## Summary

AnyData is a local-only database app. Your data stays on your device. The app does not require an account and does not sync data to developer-operated servers.

## Data we store

- Workspaces, schemas (classes, enumerables), records, views, and forms you create are stored **on your device** using on-device storage (SwiftData).
- The app does **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports on behalf of the developer in the current release.

## Data we do not collect

- No account registration or login
- No cloud backup or sync by the app itself
- No tracking for advertising
- No sale of personal data

## Export and sharing

When you choose Export (SQL DDL, INSERT SQL, or CSV), files are created on demand in app cache and shared only through the system share sheet that **you** initiate. The developer does not receive those files.

## Third parties

The app’s core features do not transmit your database contents to third parties. Standard operating-system services (for example the share sheet) may process files you choose to share according to those services’ own policies.

## Children’s privacy

The app does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
