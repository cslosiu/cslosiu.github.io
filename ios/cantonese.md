## 功能詳解

### 1. 中文查詢

- 在主畫面輸入句子或單字後搜尋。
- App 會從輸入中擷取漢字，並逐字查詢。
- 結果以列表顯示；點選字元即可進入詳情頁。

### 2. 粵音讀法與播放

- 讀音資料來自香港中文大學《粵音字典》（LexiCan）。
- 同一字若有多個讀音，會一併列出。
- 點選讀音按鈕即可播放對應音檔。

### 3. 同音字

- 在字元詳情頁瀏覽與該粵音相同的字。
- 適合寫作、校對，以及對照相近字形。

### 4. 重編國語辭典釋義

- 擷取臺灣教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》釋義。
- 在粵音之外補充書面語意思。
- 需要同時核對口語粵音與書面義時特別方便。

### 5. 香港小學生字詞

- 資料來自教育局《小學學習字詞表》。
- 與該字相關的詞語按**第一學習階段**、**第二學習階段**分組。
- 每項顯示普通話與粵語拼音。
- 點選「普」或「粵」即可播放；多音節會依序播放。
- 詞語以可換行的標籤列出，方便一眼睇晒；點選詞語展開拼音。靠近底部展開時會自動捲動，避免被遮住。

### 6. 筆順示範

- 若該字有筆順資料，詳情頁會出現「筆順」區塊。
- 點選「查看筆順」進入獨立全高頁面。
- 以 WebView 載入教育局筆順示範（播放、暫停、格線等依官方頁面提供的控制）。

### 7. 查詢紀錄

- 保留近期查詢。
- 聚焦輸入框時顯示紀錄標籤；點選即可再次查詢。

### 8. 詳情頁複製功能

右上角「⋯」選單：

- **複製讀音** — 所有讀音字串，以空格分隔
- **複製同音字** — 所有同音字，字與字之間無空格
- **複製釋義** — 所有釋義行，以換行分隔

### 9. 裝置端快取

- 盡量快取字典 HTML、讀音檔、教育局字詞 HTML／音檔等。
- 之後查詢會先讀快取，需要時再向網路更新，重複查詢更快、更省流量。

### 10. 支援裝置

- 原生 iOS App，支援 iPhone 與 iPad。
- 介面提供英文及繁體中文相關地區語系（包括 zh-HK、zh-TW、zh-MO、zh-Hant）。

## Features in detail

### 1. Chinese text search

- Type a sentence or single characters on the home screen, then search.
- The app extracts Chinese characters from your input and looks each one up.
- Results appear as a list; tap a character to open its detail page.

### 2. Cantonese pronunciation with audio

- Pronunciations come from the Chinese University of Hong Kong’s Cantonese dictionary (LexiCan).
- Multiple readings for the same character are shown when available.
- Tap a reading chip to play the corresponding sound file.

### 3. Same-sound characters (homophones)

- On the character detail page, browse characters that share the same Cantonese reading.
- Useful for writing, proofreading, and comparing similar forms.

### 4. Revised Mandarin Dictionary definitions

- Pulls definition lines from Taiwan’s Revised Mandarin Dictionary (MOE).
- Complements Cantonese readings with written-Chinese meaning.
- Helps when you need both spoken Cantonese and standard written sense.

### 5. Hong Kong primary-school lexical items

- Based on the Education Bureau’s Primary School Chinese Lexical Items list.
- Phrases related to the character are grouped by **Learning Stage 1** and **Learning Stage 2**.
- Each item shows Mandarin and Cantonese pinyin.
- Tap the Mandarin or Cantonese pinyin to play audio (multi-syllable phrases play in sequence).
- Words are shown as chips in a wrapping layout so you can scan the whole stage at a glance; tap a word to expand its pinyin panel. Expanding near the bottom auto-scrolls so the panel stays visible.

### 6. Stroke-order demonstration

- When stroke-order data is available for a character, a **Stroke Order** section appears on the detail page.
- Tap **Check the stroke ordering** to open a dedicated full-height screen.
- The screen loads the Education Bureau stroke-order demo in a WebView (play, pause, and grid options as provided by the official page).

### 7. Query history

- Recent searches are kept as query history.
- Focus the search field to see history chips; tap one to run that query again.

### 8. Copy tools on the detail page

From the top-right menu (⋯):

- **Copy Sound** — all pronunciation strings, separated by spaces
- **Copy Same-Sound Characters** — all homophones, with no spaces between characters
- **Copy Explanation** — all definition lines, separated by newlines

### 9. On-device caching

- Lexicon HTML, pronunciation audio, EDB phrase HTML/audio, and related assets are cached when possible.
- Later lookups prefer cache first, then refresh from the network when needed—so repeat searches feel faster and use less data.

### 10. iPhone & iPad

- Built as a native iOS app for iPhone and iPad.
- Localized UI strings for English and Traditional Chinese regions (including zh-HK, zh-TW, zh-MO, zh-Hant).
