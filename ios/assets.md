## Positioning

Assets is a personal wealth organizer for people who hold money in more than one place and more than one currency. Build sections that match how you think—Cash, Brokerage, Property, Crypto, Liabilities—then see section sums and a grand total in the currency you choose.

---

## Key benefits

### One total, many currencies
Enter amounts in the currency they actually use. Assets converts section totals and your overall total using up-to-date exchange rates, so you are not doing spreadsheet math in your head.

### Live stock positions
Add Yahoo Finance tickers, set your share count (including fractional units), and keep market value in sync. Quotes refresh when you return to the main list and while you stay on screen.

### See the mix
Open the distribution chart to understand how each category contributes to your portfolio—ratios, percentages, and a net total at a glance.

### Built for real life
Negative amounts for debts and liabilities, light and dark appearance, reorder sections and items, and export or import your data as JSON when you need a backup or a fresh device.

## How it works

1. **Create sections** for the categories you care about.  
2. **Add assets**—manual amounts or stock tickers with shares.  
3. **Pick a default currency** and let totals convert automatically.  
4. **Review the chart** when you want the big-picture mix.  
5. **Export** when you want a portable backup.

## FAQ

**Is Assets free?**  
Yes. The app is free to download and shows a banner ad on the main screen.

**Do I need an account?**  
No. Everything stays on your iPhone (or iPad, if you support that target).

**Where do prices and FX rates come from?**  
Stock quotes use Yahoo Finance. Exchange rates use the Frankfurter API and are cached until you refresh or change the default currency.

**Can I track debts?**  
Yes. Enter negative amounts; they display in parentheses and in a distinct color.

**Can I move my data?**  
Use Export to share or save a JSON file, and Import to merge data back in.
