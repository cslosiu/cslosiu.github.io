# Price Watch

Compare everyday prices across Hong Kong supermarket and personal-care chains — spot a better deal before you shop.

Price Watch helps you browse and compare product prices from major local retailers. Browse categories, search by brand or name, check offers, and save favourites. Data is powered by open data and typically updated daily.

### Browse and find

- Browse thousands of daily goods in clear categories
- Filter by category and subcategory
- Search by brand or product name
- Favourite products for quick checking next time

### Compare prices and offers

- Compare prices from different shops, listed from low to high
- See discount offers for each store when available
- See when prices were last updated

### Use offline and in your language

- Use downloaded data offline; refresh anytime to update
- Light and Dark appearance
- English / Traditional Chinese UI based on your device language

### Data source

Price information comes from the Consumer Council’s Online Price Watch open data（網上價格一覽通）:

[https://online-price-watch.consumer.org.hk/opw/opendata/pricewatch.json](https://online-price-watch.consumer.org.hk/opw/opendata/pricewatch.json)

The dataset is typically updated daily and includes prices and offers from chains such as Wellcome, PARKnSHOP, Market Place by Jasons, Watsons, AEON, DCH Food Mart, and other retailers in the published data.

### Disclaimer

This app is not an official Consumer Council product. Prices and offers are shown as provided by the open data source and may differ from in-store or online prices. Always confirm the latest price and offer terms at the retailer.

## 價格一覽

比較香港各大超市及個人護理連鎖店的日常商品價格——購物前先睇邊間更抵。

「價格一覽」幫你瀏覽並比較本地主要商戶的商品價格。可分類瀏覽、以品牌或名稱搜尋、查看優惠，並收藏常用商品。資料來自開放數據，約每日更新。

### 瀏覽與搜尋

- 瀏覽數以千計日常商品，分類清楚
- 按分類及子分類篩選
- 以品牌或商品名稱搜尋
- 收藏商品，方便下次快速查看

### 比較價格與優惠

- 同一商品比較不同店舖價格，由低至高排列
- 顯示各店優惠資訊（如有）
- 顯示相對更新時間

### 離線與介面

- 已下載資料可離線瀏覽；隨時手動更新
- 支援淺色／深色外觀
- 介面跟隨裝置語言（英文／繁體中文）

### 資料來源

價格資料來自香港消費者委員會「網上價格一覽通」開放數據：

[https://online-price-watch.consumer.org.hk/opw/opendata/pricewatch.json](https://online-price-watch.consumer.org.hk/opw/opendata/pricewatch.json)

資料集約每日更新，涵蓋惠康、百佳、Market Place by Jasons、屈臣氏、AEON、大昌食品，以及數據集內其他商戶的價格與優惠。

### 免責聲明

本 App 並非消費者委員會官方應用程式。所顯示價格及優惠以開放數據為準，或與店舖／網上實際售價有所不同。購物前請向有關商戶確認最新價格及優惠條款。
